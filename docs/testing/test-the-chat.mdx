`renderChat` renders `<AgentChat>`, open, with an adapter you choose and any of its props. Use it to check what your
customizations do: your words, a theme, a replaced part, Arabic with right-to-left layout.

## A complete example

<include meta='title="chat.browser.test.tsx"'>/_examples/testing/chat.browser.test.tsx</include>

`chat.part("input")` finds an element by its `data-wa-part`, the same names you style. `chat.host` is the chat's
root, `chat.panel` its panel.

## No string outside the catalog

With `sentinel: true`, every catalog string is replaced by its key (`⟦composer.send⟧`). Any other text left on screen,
in an `aria-label`, a placeholder or a title, was written somewhere the catalog does not reach.
`toHaveNoHardcodedText` fails with each such string and where it is. List what is allowed: the person's message and
the model's answer.

Run it after you replace a part with your own component: it is how you find the label you forgot to translate.
